10.1 线代

(AB)-1=B-1A-1

tr(AB)=tr(BA)

det(AB)=det(A)det(B)

10.2 矩阵求导

∂(aTx)∂x=a

∂(xTAx)∂x=(A+AT)x

∂‖x‖22∂x=2x

∂tr(AB)∂A=BT

10.3 概率

P(A|B)=P(A∩B)P(B)

P(A|B)=P(B|A)P(A)P(B)

P(B)=∑iP(B|Ai)P(Ai)

E[aX+bY]=aE[X]+bE[Y]

Var(X)=E[X2]-(E[X])2

Cov(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]

10.4 统计与信息论

¯X=1n∑iXi

s2=1n-1∑i(Xi-¯X)2

H(p,q)=-∑ipilogqi

DKL(pVertq)=∑ipilogpiqi

H(p,q)=H(p)+DKL(pVertq)
